Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Inbox Bridge (personal) is a private tool used only by its owner to let Claude Desktop read the owner's own Gmail accounts.

What it accesses

Read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) for accounts the owner signs in with. It cannot send, delete or modify email.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Email content is fetched directly from Google when requested and is not stored on any server run by this app.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Email content requested in a chat is processed by Claude (Anthropic) as part of that conversation.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
